Delete your account and data

Last updated: 22 November 2026

You can remove your Tutorix login and stop our use of your profile data using either method below. This page is the official link for account and data deletion requests (including users who found Tutorix through Google Play or the App Store).

Option 1 — In the mobile app (fastest)

  1. Install or open the Tutorix app and sign in.
  2. Go to your profile (Account for students or tutors).
  3. Tap Delete account, confirm, and you will be signed out. Deletion is immediate for the live service.

Option 2 — Email us

If you cannot use the app, email us from your registered Tutorix email address and ask us to delete your account. We verify ownership before processing.

Request account deletion by email (info@tutorix.tech)

Include:

  • Your full name and registered email (and mobile, if you use it to sign in).
  • Whether you are a student, tutor, or both.
  • A clear statement that you want your account and associated personal data deleted.

We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days. We may reply to confirm identity or ask a clarifying question before deletion.

What gets deleted

We deactivate your login, anonymize contact details (so the same email or phone can be registered again later), and stop using your profile for the live marketplace.

Some records may be retained where the law requires it (for example payment, invoice, tax, or dispute records). Those are not used to operate your profile after deletion. See our Privacy Policy for more detail.
