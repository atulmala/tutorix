Refunds & Cancellations

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Students buy tutoring classes on Tutorix from individual tutors. All prices are shown and charged in Indian Rupees (INR, ₹), inclusive of the platform and payment charges shown at checkout. This policy explains when you can cancel classes and how refunds are paid.

Cancelling classes before they happen

  • You can cancel any class you have paid for but not yet scheduled.
  • You can cancel a scheduled class up to 15 minutes before an online class starts, or up to 30 minutes before an offline (in-person) class starts. After that the class can no longer be cancelled.
  • Cancel from Booking history in the Tutorix app or website. The refund amount is shown before you confirm.
  • If you bought several classes at a bulk-discount price and cancel some of them, the classes you keep are repriced at the discount tier that applies to the smaller number, and the refund is the difference.
  • Free or demo classes are not refundable.

Refunds after a class

If a class did not take place, had technical problems, or you were not satisfied, you can raise a refund claim from the concluded class in your booking history until 11:59 PM IST on the day after the class. The tutor is asked to respond and Tutorix reviews every claim before approving or rejecting it. You can follow the status (Under process, Approved, Rejected, Settled) on the class card.

How refunds are paid

  • Tutorix wallet: the amount is credited to your wallet after processing and can be used for future bookings.
  • Original payment method: the refund is initiated to the UPI, card, or bank account you paid with within 2 working days. Your bank may take a further 5–7 working days to show it.
  • Class credit: for post-class claims (except “not satisfied”), you can choose to get the class back to reschedule with the same tutor.

We email you when your refund is complete.

Fees

Registration fees are non-refundable once paid, except where charged in error or where required by law. Payments are processed by our payment partners Razorpay and Cashfree.
